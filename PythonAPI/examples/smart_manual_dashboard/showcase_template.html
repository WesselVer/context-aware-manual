MOMENTS IN THE DRIVE · • A TIP SHOWS

    HOW THE MANUAL PICKS ITS MOMENT

    A good moment

    • Standing still for 4 s
    • Steady speed on a straight for 6 s
    • A quiet road: one car or none within 30 m

    Held back when

    • A junction is coming up (4 s ahead)
    • 3 or more cars within 30 m
    • A manoeuvre: turning, reversing, indicating, braking hard, and for 3 s after

    Beginner curriculum

    • Cruise control on and off
    • Brake to switch it off, after using it a while
    • Adaptive cruise, a long press
    • Autopilot, after using ACC a while

    Running a session

    • B E P start Beginner, Experienced or Expert
    • Hold R to switch the manual off
    • Something already done before its tip is skipped
    • Experienced and Expert tips are next
